## Prompt final

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: a single polished raster app icon for the JK bubble on a dark personal website, displayed at only 58–80 pixels. Primary request: frontal circular metallic medallion, deep navy and slate face, a warm gold rim and a restrained cyan edge highlight. Central bold readable interlocking monogram with the exact letters "JK" (J K), large and unmistakable. Subtle glass-and-metal finish reminiscent of refined early desktop interface icons, clean and modern, not an ornate badge. Composition: centered, symmetrical frontal view, circle occupies approximately 84 percent of a square canvas, with about 8 percent clear padding per side. Truly transparent outside the circle, no colored background, no fake checkerboard, no scene. High contrast, broad shapes, crisp outer silhouette, few small details, legible at thumbnail size. No text except JK; no slogan, no lock, no shield, no decorations, no watermark.
