## Prompt final

Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished abstract circular symbol for the JK bubble in a personal portfolio desktop website. Pure black and white monochrome, clean flat vector-like contours, bold interwoven flowing ribbons with a subtle organic thorn-like rhythm arranged into a balanced round vortex or orbital knot. The letters J and K may be subtly suggested by the intertwined geometry, but no explicit typography or readable text is necessary. Sophisticated, distinctive, minimal, legible at 64 pixels. Circular overall silhouette like a bubble icon, centered with small even padding on a square canvas. Crucial: genuinely transparent background, and large transparent negative-space openings throughout the interior between the black and white strokes, so moving colorful GIF wallpaper will be visible THROUGH the icon. No filled background disc, no solid badge plate, no square backing, no checkerboard drawn into the art, no color, no gold, no gradients, no shadows or glow, no mockup. Only the black and white interwoven mark exists on alpha transparency.
