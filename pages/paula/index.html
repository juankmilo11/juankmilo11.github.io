BEFORE WE MEET

GOOD TO
KNOW.

How do I confirm my appointment?+

Upload your inspiration design, complete the form and submit your request online. You will receive a request number. Once Paula Daniela contacts you on WhatsApp, you must pay a minimum $20 USD deposit to confirm the appointment.

Can I bring a reference?+

Yes! Upload an inspiration design photo with every request, even if you selected a catalog reference. Tell Paula what you love and what you would change.

How much does a service cost?+

Pricing depends on the service and the details of your idea. Ask for a quote before confirming. Photos show completed work, rather than fixed-price packages.

Where is my appointment?+

Paula works in New Jersey. Confirm the location and directions for your appointment by message.

Need to change your request?+

You can edit your details before submitting. After submitting, contact Paula on WhatsApp. Cancellations or rescheduling require at least 1 day’s notice; same-day changes are not allowed.
