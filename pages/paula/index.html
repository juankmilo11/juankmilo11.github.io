BEFORE WE MEET

GOOD TO
KNOW.

How do I confirm my appointment?+

Complete the form and submit your request online. You will receive a request number. Paula will contact you on WhatsApp to confirm the appointment.

Can I bring a reference?+

Absolutely! Choose a piece from the catalog or add an inspiration photo to your request. Tell Paula what you love and what you’d change to make the design yours.

How much does a service cost?+

Pricing depends on the service and the details of your idea. Ask for a quote before confirming. Photos show completed work, rather than fixed-price packages.

Where is my appointment?+

Paula works in New Jersey. Confirm the location and directions for your appointment by message.

Need to change your request?+

You can edit your details before submitting. After submitting, contact Paula on WhatsApp to arrange changes to your saved request.
