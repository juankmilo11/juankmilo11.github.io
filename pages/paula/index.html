How do I confirm my appointment?+
Fill out the form, copy the summary and send it to @paulaadaniiela on Instagram. The request does not automatically reserve a spot: arrange the date, time and details with Paula.
Can I bring a reference?+
Absolutely! Choose a piece from the catalog or share an image on Instagram. Tell Paula what you love and what you’d change to make the design yours.
How much does a service cost?+
Pricing depends on the service and the details of your idea. Ask for a quote before confirming. Photos show completed work, rather than fixed-price packages.
Where is my appointment?+
Paula works in New Jersey. Confirm the location and directions for your appointment by message.
Need to change your request?+
You can edit your details before copying the request. If you’ve already sent it on Instagram, message Paula in the same conversation to arrange any changes.
